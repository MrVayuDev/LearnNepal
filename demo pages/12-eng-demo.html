1.[2081 Koshi Q.N. 1]Sentence Completion (Vocabulary in Context)
2082 NEB 5 Marks Short Answer

Complete the following sentences using the correct words highlighted in the unseen passage:

a. World leaders are now united in their ______ for global peace.
b. People are impressed by her ______ manner and relaxed style of public writing.
c. As I started to live on my own, I ______ myself in my professional career.
d. The stuff written about me helped to establish me as a/an ______ figure in public life.
e. His desire for money is completely ______.
chevron_right View Solution & Marking Rubric
Standard Model Answers (NEB 2082 Marking Guide) 1 Mark each
  • a. quest / commitment / crusade
  • b. genial / dazzling / amicable
  • c. immersed / absorbed
  • d. esteemed / venerable / towering
  • e. insatiable / voracious

Examiner Note: Exact synonyms derived from paragraph 2 are awarded full credit. Minor spelling variations permitted if phonetically uncompromised.

3.[2080 Gandaki Q.N. 3]Comprehension Analytical Short Questions
2082 NEB 5 Marks Descriptive

Answer the following textual questions in concise, grammatically coherent sentences:

  1. Why did Sarah go to the library in the beginning?
  2. What happened when Sarah reads the book about a boy who can fly?
  3. What was special about the book "The Lost Amulet of Althoria"?
  4. What was Alaric searching for in the tale?
  5. Was Sarah more determined to read books at the end? Give a valid textual reason.
chevron_right View Model Answers

a. Motivation for visiting library:

Sarah visited the library seeking solace and escape from her monotonous daily routine, attracted by the sanctuary of ancient chronicles.

b. Sensory experience while reading:

She experienced vivid sensory illusions where the classroom faded and she felt tangible gusts of wind lifting her off the stone floor alongside the protagonist.

c. Special property of the book:

The text was alive; illuminated script rearranged dynamically depending on the emotional resonance of the reader holding the leather-bound pages.

4.[2081 Madhesh Q.N. 4]
Neighbours by Tim WintonShort Story
2080 NEB 2 Marks

How would you react if you were in the young man's situation at the end of the story? (Neighbours)

chevron_right View Model Answer & Theme Analysis

If I were in the young man's position at the conclusion of Tim Winton's "Neighbours", I would feel an overwhelming mixture of humility, relief, and profound emotional awakening. Initially prejudiced against the boisterous European immigrant neighborhood, his final realization through weeping demonstrates that human solidarity transcends cultural barriers.

Key Marks Indicator: Mention of intercultural empathy, breakdown of intellectual arrogance, and emotional solidarity during childbirth.

Option 1 • A Respectable Woman

How was Gouvernail different from Mrs. Baroda's expectation?

OR
Option 2 • A Devoted Son

Do you think Rakesh was a devoted son? Explain with references from the story.

chevron_right View Both Model Answers

For "A Respectable Woman":

10.[2081 Lumbini Q.N. 10]
Poetry Context & Stanza ParaphrasePoetry
2082 NEB 5 Marks

Explain the following lines of the poem 'A Day' by Emily Dickinson with reference to context:

"The hills untied their bonnets,
The bobolinks begun.
Then I said softly to myself,
That must have been the sun!"
chevron_right View Critical Explanation & Poetic Devices

Context: These lines are extracted from Emily Dickinson's celebrated nature poem 'A Day', which describes sunrise and sunset through innocent childlike observation.

Poetic Devices: Personification (hills untying bonnets like cheerful village maidens removing their morning hoods as morning light dispels shadows). Imagery and Auditory appeal through the singing bobolinks.

Significance: Represents awakening, natural delight, and unpretentious discovery of daylight.

11.[2082 Bagmati Q.N. 11]Data Interpretation & Comparative Analysis
2082 NEB 7 Marks Long Answer

Study the following survey table showing Internet Usage Trends Among Secondary Students in Nepal (2020 vs 2025) and interpret the information in about 150 words. Make comparisons where necessary.

Primary Purpose 2020 (%) 2025 (%) Trend Direction
Online Classes & Research 24% 58% trending_up Sharp Rise (+34%)
Social Media Networking 45% 26% trending_down Significant Decline (-19%)
Online Gaming & Video Streaming 21% 11% trending_down Moderated Use (-10%)
Skill Dev & Coding Portals 10% 15% trending_up Steady Growth (+5%)
chevron_right View 150-Word Model Interpretation

The provided dataset portrays a noticeable shift in internet utilization patterns among Nepali secondary school students between 2020 and 2025. Most strikingly, educational browsing—encompassing online classes and academic research—surged more than two-fold from 24% to 58%, marking a clear transformation into an academic tool.

Conversely, leisure activities experienced a downward trajectory. Social media engagement dropped dramatically from 45% to 26%, while gaming and video streaming saw a 10% decline down to 11%. Independent skill acquisition and coding portals gained ground, advancing from 10% to 15%. In summary, Nepali youth have pivoted from passive entertainment toward productive digital scholarship over the five-year timeframe.

13.[2081 Sudurpashchim Q.N. 13]Expository Essay Writing (300 Words)
2080 NEB 10 Marks

"Walking is not just a physical activity. It has manifold holistic benefits." Write an essay on walking as a physical activity and its role in fostering physical vitality, cognitive clarity, and societal harmony.

chevron_right View Suggested Outline & High-Scoring Vocabulary

Paragraph 1 (Intro): Introduce walking as the most natural, egalitarian form of human mobility and cardiovascular wellness.

Paragraph 2 (Physical & Mental): Endorphin secretion, alleviation of stress, reduction in chronic lifestyle risks (hypertension, metabolic disorders).

Paragraph 3 (Social Connection): Strolling in neighborhoods encourages community interactions, decreases motorized traffic congestion, and reclaims public spaces.

Paragraph 4 (Conclusion): Synthesis: A call to integrate daily walks into our tech-bound sedentary lives.

[2082 Koshi Q.N. 14]Question 14 • Adjective Identification
2082 1 Mark

Health care in Nepal is not as expensive as it is in the US. (Identify the adjective in the sentence.)

arrow_right Solution
Answer: expensive
Rule: 'Expensive' functions as a predicate adjective modifying the noun phrase 'Health care in Nepal' within the comparative construction 'as ... as'.
[2082 Madhesh Q.N. 15]Question 15 • Gerund vs Infinitive
2082 1 Mark

My car needs ______ right now. (repair / repairing / to repair / repaired)

arrow_right Solution
Answer: repairing (or: to be repaired)
Rule: The verb 'need' when followed by an inanimate object having something done to it takes either a Gerund with passive meaning (needs repairing) or passive infinitive (needs to be repaired).
[2082 Bagmati Q.N. 16]Question 16 • Relative Clauses
2082 1 Mark

This is the house. We spent our childhood in the house. (Join the sentences using an appropriate relative pronoun.)

arrow_right Solution
Answer: This is the house where we spent our childhood.
Alternative: This is the house in which we spent our childhood.
[2082 Gandaki Q.N. 17]Question 17 • Subject-Verb Agreement
2082 1 Mark

Sumsher along with his friends ______ me well. (know / knows / have known / knowing)

arrow_right Solution
Answer: knows
Rule: When subjects are joined by phrases like 'along with', 'as well as', or 'accompanied by', the verb agrees with the first subject ('Sumsher' - singular).
[2082 Lumbini Q.N. 18]Question 18 • Prepositions
2082 1 Mark

arrow_right Solution
