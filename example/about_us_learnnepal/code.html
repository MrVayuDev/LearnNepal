Our Story

LearnNepal was born out of a simple observation: students in Nepal, especially those in Grades 10, 11, and 12, often struggle to find comprehensive, digital-first resources that align with the NEB syllabus.

Our founders—a group of passionate educators and tech enthusiasts—witnessed the digital divide first-hand. While some had access to expensive coaching, many were left behind. We set out to bridge this gap by creating a centralized, free, and interactive hub for learning.
